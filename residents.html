Community Guidelines

Driving

The posted speed limit is 10 mph. Please drive slowly on our narrow, winding roads.

When two vehicles meet on a section too narrow to pass, the uphill driver has the right-of-way. The downhill driver has greater control in reverse and should back up to the nearest pull-out or driveway. The uphill driver may choose to yield if they are close to a pull-out and feel safe doing so, but it is at their discretion.

Cycling & Walking

Cyclists should ride with the flow of traffic, not against it.

Pedestrians and runners should travel against traffic so you can see oncoming vehicles. On blind corners with little or no shoulder, move to the outside edge of the road for greater visibility and to avoid being trapped against a hillside or fence.

Parking

Use your own driveway or property first. If you expect more vehicles than you have spaces for — such as for a gathering — ask neighbors in advance before using their spots.

If a delivery or home project may require a large truck or temporarily block the road, please notify neighbors ahead of time, or email the board so they can send out a notice.

Pets & Wildlife

Please leash all pets during walks and be courteous when encountering other animals on the road. Carry bags for waste and dispose of them in your own garbage bin — not your neighbor's.

Bobcats and mountain lions roam this area. Use caution when leaving animals outdoors unattended.
