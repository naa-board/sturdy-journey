January 2025 Board Meeting

Saturday, January 18, 2025  ·  10:00 am  ·  Held via video call

Attendees

  • Board members: Pat Souza (chair), Dana Ellroy, Marcus Fenn, Yuki Tanaka
  • Residents in attendance: 4

2025 Budget Review

  • Board reviewed proposed 2025 operating budget of $28,000.
  • Primary line items: road maintenance ($18,000), insurance ($4,500), administrative ($1,500), reserve contribution ($4,000).
  • Budget approved unanimously.

Annual Dues

  • Annual dues set at $350 per parcel for 2025, unchanged from 2024.
  • Due date: February 1, 2025.
  • Yuki to send dues notice to all 24 parcels by January 20.

Road Inspection

  • Marcus conducted a road inspection in December. Full report shared with board.
  • Upper section shows wear consistent with last winter's rainfall; recommend patching in spring.
  • Culvert at mile marker 0.4 is functioning but should be monitored through rainy season.

Insurance Renewal

  • Policy renewed with same carrier. Premium increased 6% over 2024.
  • Coverage limits remain unchanged.

Action Items

  • Yuki: Send annual dues notice to all parcels by January 20
  • Marcus: Monitor culvert at 0.4 mile and report at March meeting
  • Dana: Confirm insurance certificate on file with county

Next Meeting

March 15, 2025 at 10:00 am via video call.
