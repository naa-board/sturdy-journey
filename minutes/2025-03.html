March 2025 Board Meeting

Saturday, March 15, 2025  ·  10:00 am  ·  Held via video call

Attendees

  • Board members: Pat Souza (chair), Dana Ellroy, Marcus Fenn, Yuki Tanaka
  • Residents in attendance: 6

Road Condition Update

Marcus presented the post-winter damage assessment. Two sections of the road surface show significant cracking and two potholes have opened on the upper switchback. Overall road condition is fair.

Contractor Bids — Spring Patching

  • Three bids received for spring pothole patching work.
  • Board voted 4–0 to accept the bid from Valley Road Services ($2,400) for pothole repair during the week of April 21.
  • Work to be scheduled for weekday mornings to minimize impact on residents.

Storm Cleanup Cost Recovery

  • February storm cleanup cost $1,850, partially covered by reserves.
  • Board discussed whether a special assessment is needed; tabled until fall meeting pending review of full-year expenses.

Treasurer's Report

  • Current balance: $14,230
  • Dues collection: 18 of 24 parcels paid as of meeting date
  • Dana to send reminder to remaining 6 parcels by end of March

Action Items

  • Marcus: Confirm contractor start date and notify neighbors by email
  • Dana: Send dues reminder to outstanding parcels
  • Pat: Post spring patching announcement to website

Next Meeting

The next board meeting will be held in June 2025. Date to be announced by email.
