October 2024 Board Meeting

Saturday, October 19, 2024  ·  10:00 am  ·  Held via video call

Attendees

  • Board members: Pat Souza (chair), Dana Ellroy, Marcus Fenn
  • Residents in attendance: 7

Summer Road Work — Final Report

  • Chip seal resurfacing of the lower 0.6 miles completed in August by Pacific Coast Paving.
  • Total project cost: $22,400 (within budget).
  • Road is performing well; no issues reported by residents since completion.

Drainage Improvements

  • Two new drainage swales were added as part of the summer project.
  • Board to monitor performance through the upcoming rainy season.

Winter Preparedness

  • Contractor on standby for storm debris clearance (same arrangement as 2023–24).
  • Residents reminded to report fallen trees or road hazards promptly to board email.
  • Board confirmed emergency contact protocol: email board@aldercroftheights.org for non-urgent issues; call 911 for road emergencies blocking access.

Year-End Financials Preview

  • Current balance: $16,580 after summer project.
  • Reserve target of $15,000 maintained.
  • Full year-end financial summary will be presented at the January 2025 meeting.

Action Items

  • Marcus: Send winter preparedness reminder to all residents by November 1
  • Pat: Confirm standby contract with storm debris contractor
  • Dana: Prepare year-end financial summary for January meeting

Next Meeting

January 18, 2025 at 10:00 am via video call.
