About the Association

The North Aldercroft Association is a voluntary neighborhood organization serving property owners and residents along North Aldercroft Heights Road. We coordinate road maintenance, share information about local conditions, and work together on issues that affect our community.

The roots of the neighborhood go back to the late 1800s, when North Aldercroft Heights was part of a large family ranch. In the 1920s, the land was subdivided into small lots — the beginnings of the community we live in today. A road improvement association formed early to pool resources for maintaining roads that fall outside Santa Clara County jurisdiction because of the road's steep and narrow grade.

The North Aldercroft Association, Inc. was formally incorporated on August 10, 1967, succeeding the earlier unincorporated group known as the Locust Road Association. The association is governed by a volunteer board that meets at least four times per year. Meeting minutes and announcements are posted on this site.
