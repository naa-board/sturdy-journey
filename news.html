Announcements

  • April 14, 2025
    Spring Pothole Patching — Week of April 21
    A contractor will be filling potholes along the upper section of North Aldercroft Heights Road during the week of April 21. Expect brief single-lane delays between 9 am and 3 pm. No road closures are planned.

    Winter Storm Debris Cleared
    The slide debris from the February storms has been cleared. Thank you to everyone who helped coordinate the cleanup effort. A report on costs and the road condition assessment will be discussed at the March board meeting.
  • January 6, 2025
    2025 Annual Dues — Due February 1
    Annual road maintenance dues of $350 per parcel are due by February 1, 2025. Please send a check payable to "North Aldercroft Association" or contact the board for other payment arrangements.
