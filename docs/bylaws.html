Bylaws

North Aldercroft Association

Adopted pursuant to the Articles of Incorporation

Article I — Name and Principal Office

The name of this corporation is North Aldercroft Association (the "Association"). The principal office of the Association is located in Santa Clara County, California.

Article II — Purposes

The Association is organized to manage, maintain, repair, and improve North Aldercroft Heights Road, a private road in Santa Clara County, for the benefit of all property owners whose parcels are accessed by or encumbered by an easement over said road. The Association is authorized to collect road maintenance dues, enter into contracts, and take any other action in furtherance of this purpose.

Article III — Membership

Section 3.1 — Eligibility. Every owner of a parcel of real property that is accessed by or encumbered by an easement over North Aldercroft Heights Road is a member of the Association by operation of law and by the terms of the court order establishing the Association's dues authority.

Section 3.2 — Transfer of Membership. Membership is appurtenant to ownership of a qualifying parcel and automatically transfers to any successor in interest upon conveyance of the property. No member may assign, transfer, or waive membership separately from ownership of the parcel.

Section 3.3 — Dues Obligation. Each member is obligated to pay annual road maintenance dues in the amounts set forth in the court order or as otherwise lawfully adjusted by the Board. Unpaid dues constitute a lien on the member's parcel.

Article IV — Board of Directors

Section 4.1 — Number. The Board of Directors shall consist of not fewer than three (3) and not more than seven (7) directors. The number may be changed by amendment of these Bylaws or by resolution of the Board, subject to the foregoing limits.

Section 4.2 — Qualifications. Each director must be a member of the Association or a designee of a member entity that is a property owner.

Section 4.3 — Election and Term. Directors are elected at the annual meeting of members by a plurality vote. Each director serves a term of two (2) years or until a successor is elected and qualified. Terms shall be staggered to provide continuity.

Section 4.4 — Vacancies. Any vacancy on the Board may be filled by a majority vote of the remaining directors. A director appointed to fill a vacancy serves the remainder of the unexpired term.

Section 4.5 — Compensation. Directors serve without compensation. The Board may authorize reimbursement of reasonable expenses incurred in the performance of Board duties.

Article V — Meetings of the Board

Section 5.1 — Regular Meetings. The Board shall hold at least two (2) regular meetings per calendar year. Meetings may be held in person or by any means of remote communication that permits all participants to communicate simultaneously.

Section 5.2 — Special Meetings. Special meetings of the Board may be called by the Chair or by any two directors, upon at least 48 hours notice to all directors.

Section 5.3 — Quorum. A majority of the authorized number of directors constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business. Every act or decision made by a majority of the directors present at a duly held meeting at which a quorum is present is the act of the Board.

Section 5.4 — Member Attendance. Regular Board meetings shall be open to all members of the Association. The Board may hold executive sessions from which non-directors are excluded when discussing matters of a legally sensitive nature.

Article VI — Officers

Section 6.1 — Officers. The officers of the Association shall be a Chair, a Secretary, and a Treasurer. The Board may appoint additional officers as it deems appropriate. One person may hold more than one office, except that no person shall serve as both Chair and Secretary.

Section 6.2 — Election and Term. Officers are elected by the Board at its first meeting following the annual election of directors, and serve at the pleasure of the Board.

Section 6.3 — Duties. The Chair presides at all meetings and exercises general supervisory authority over the affairs of the Association. The Secretary maintains the corporate records and minutes. The Treasurer is responsible for the financial records, annual budget, dues billing, and financial reporting to the membership.

Article VII — Meetings of Members

Section 7.1 — Annual Meeting. An annual meeting of members shall be held each year at a time and place determined by the Board. The purpose of the annual meeting is to elect directors, receive financial and operational reports, and transact such other business as may properly come before the meeting.

Section 7.2 — Notice. Written notice of any member meeting shall be given to each member not less than ten (10) nor more than sixty (60) days prior to the meeting. Notice may be delivered by mail or email to the address on file with the Association.

Section 7.3 — Quorum. Members representing twenty-five percent (25%) of the parcels shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of members.

Section 7.4 — Voting. Each parcel is entitled to one vote on any matter submitted to a vote of members. Where a parcel is owned by multiple persons, those persons shall designate a single voter.

Article VIII — Dues and Assessments

Section 8.1 — Authority. The Association's authority to levy and collect road maintenance dues is established by court order entered in the Superior Court of the State of California, County of Santa Clara, in the 1970s pursuant to California private road law. That court order, and the dues structure appended thereto, is incorporated herein by reference.

Section 8.2 — Annual Dues. Annual dues are levied on each parcel in accordance with the dues structure established by the court order. The amount varies by parcel based on distance from Aldercroft Heights Road. Current annual dues range from $700 to $1,200 per parcel.

Section 8.3 — Billing and Payment. Dues are billed annually. Payment is due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. The Board may establish a reasonable late fee for overdue dues.

Section 8.4 — Liens. Unpaid dues, together with any accrued late fees, constitute a lien on the delinquent parcel and are enforceable in accordance with California law. The Board is authorized to record a notice of lien and to pursue collection through legal means.

Section 8.5 — Sale of Property. Dues obligations run with the land. Any outstanding dues must be satisfied at or before the close of escrow on any sale or transfer of a parcel. Realtors and escrow agents should contact the Association to confirm dues status prior to closing.

Article IX — Road Maintenance

Section 9.1 — Responsibility. The Association is responsible for the maintenance, repair, and improvement of North Aldercroft Heights Road. This includes but is not limited to: road surface maintenance and repair, drainage infrastructure, culverts, erosion control, and storm damage remediation.

Section 9.2 — Contractor Selection. The Board is authorized to enter into contracts for road work on behalf of the Association. For contracts exceeding a threshold set by the Board from time to time, the Board shall solicit at least two competitive bids.

Section 9.3 — Emergency Repairs. In the event of an emergency requiring immediate road repair to preserve safe access, the Chair or any two directors may authorize emergency expenditures without a prior Board vote, subject to ratification at the next Board meeting.

Article X — Financial Management

Section 10.1 — Fiscal Year. The fiscal year of the Association shall begin on January 1 and end on December 31 of each year.

Section 10.2 — Budget. The Board shall adopt an annual operating budget prior to the beginning of each fiscal year. The budget shall be made available to members upon request.

Section 10.3 — Reserves. The Board shall maintain a reserve fund adequate to address major road repairs and capital improvements. The target reserve level shall be set by the Board and reviewed annually.

Section 10.4 — Financial Records. The Treasurer shall maintain accurate financial records and prepare a financial report for each regular Board meeting. An annual financial summary shall be distributed to all members.

Article XI — Amendments

These Bylaws may be amended by an affirmative vote of two-thirds (2/3) of the directors present at any duly held Board meeting at which a quorum is present, provided that notice of the proposed amendment was included in the notice of the meeting; or by an affirmative vote of a majority of the members present at any duly held member meeting at which a quorum is present. No amendment may conflict with the Articles of Incorporation or with the court order establishing the Association's dues authority.

Article XII — Dissolution

The Association may be dissolved only upon approval of two-thirds (2/3) of the members. Upon dissolution, after payment of all liabilities, any remaining assets shall be distributed in accordance with California law. The road maintenance obligations of property owners along North Aldercroft Heights Road are established by court order and survive dissolution of the Association.
