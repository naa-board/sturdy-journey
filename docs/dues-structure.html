Dues Structure

Annual Road Maintenance Dues

Established by court order, Santa Clara County Superior Court, 1970s  ·  Based on California private road law

Basis for Assessment

Annual road maintenance dues are levied on each parcel pursuant to a court order entered in the Santa Clara County Superior Court in the 1970s. The court order was issued under California private road law and establishes the obligation to pay as a covenant running with the land — meaning it applies to every successive owner of each parcel.

Dues are assessed based on each parcel's distance from Aldercroft Heights Road, reflecting the relative use each property makes of the private road. Parcels farther from the public road use a greater length of the private road and are assessed at a higher rate.

Current Annual Dues by Zone

The following table reflects current annual assessments. Exact parcel assignments are defined in the court order. Contact the board if you are unsure which zone applies to a specific parcel.

Zone Distance from Aldercroft Heights Rd Annual Dues
Zone 1 Nearest parcels $700
Zone 2 Mid-road parcels $900
Zone 3 Upper-road parcels $1,100
Zone 4 Farthest parcels $1,200

Payment and Billing

Dues invoices are mailed annually, typically in January. Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date. To pay or to confirm your parcel's assessment, contact the board at board@aldercroftheights.org.

Unpaid Dues and Liens

Unpaid dues constitute a lien on the parcel. Outstanding balances must be resolved at or before the close of any escrow involving the property. Realtors should contact the board before closing to confirm that no dues are in arrears.

Questions

For questions about the dues structure, your parcel's specific assessment, or the underlying court order, contact the board at board@aldercroftheights.org.
