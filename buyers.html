For Buyers & Realtors

Living on a Private Road

North Aldercroft Heights Road is a private road — meaning it is not maintained by Santa Clara County or any other public agency. The residents who live along the road are solely responsible for its upkeep, including paving, drainage, pothole repair, storm damage, and any other improvements.

This is a meaningful distinction from most neighborhoods. If you purchase property here, you are joining a community of neighbors who collectively manage and fund a shared piece of infrastructure. There is no option to opt out, and dues are a legal obligation that runs with the property.

Road Dues

Road maintenance dues were established in the 1970s by court order under California private road law. The obligation to pay is attached to each parcel — it transfers automatically to any new owner at sale.

Annual dues are assessed based on a parcel's distance from Aldercroft Heights Road, reflecting each property's relative use of the road.

Current annual dues
$700 – $1,200 per year

Exact amount varies by parcel. Contact the board for the assessment that applies to a specific property.

Dues are invoiced annually. Unpaid dues become a lien on the property and must be resolved before or at the close of escrow. Buyers should confirm the dues status of any property before purchasing.

Information for Realtors

If you are representing a buyer or seller on North Aldercroft Heights Road, please contact the board before closing. We can confirm:

  • The annual dues amount for the specific parcel
  • Whether any dues are outstanding or in arrears
  • Any pending assessments or upcoming road work that may affect costs

We recommend reaching out at least two weeks before the anticipated close date to allow time for any outstanding balances to be resolved.
