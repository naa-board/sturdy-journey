Board Update February 28, 2025

Storm Cleanup — What Happened

What Happened

The storms on February 18–20 brought heavy rainfall and triggered a small debris slide that blocked approximately 40 feet of the road near the upper switchback. The road was impassable for about 14 hours on February 19 until the debris was cleared.

How It Was Handled

Pat reached our on-call contractor at 7 am on February 19. A crew arrived by mid-morning and cleared the debris by 2 pm that day. A follow-up inspection found no structural damage to the road surface beneath the slide. The drainage swale above that section was also cleared of debris as a precaution.

Cost

The cleanup cost $1,850, which was paid from the association's reserve fund. This is within the range we budget for storm response in a typical wet year. The board will assess whether the reserve needs replenishment at the March meeting.

What to Do If You See a Problem

If you observe a road hazard, fallen tree, or anything that affects safe access, please email the board immediately at board@aldercroftheights.org. For an emergency that requires immediate response, call 911 — don't wait for the board to act.
