Meeting Summary January 22, 2025

January 2025 Board Meeting

This is an AI-generated summary of the board meeting. For the complete official record, see the full meeting minutes.

The Short Version

The board approved the 2025 budget, held dues steady at last year's rates, and flagged two road sections to watch through the winter. Insurance renewed with a small premium increase.

2025 Budget

The board approved an operating budget of $28,000 for 2025. The biggest line item is road maintenance at $18,000. The budget also includes $4,500 for insurance, $1,500 for administrative costs, and a $4,000 contribution to the road reserve fund. No surprises here — this is in line with recent years.

Dues Are the Same as Last Year

Annual dues are unchanged at $700–$1,200 per parcel (depending on your distance from Aldercroft Heights Road). Invoices went out in mid-January. Payment is due by February 1. If you haven't received your invoice or have questions about your parcel's amount, email board@aldercroftheights.org.

Road Condition Watch

The December road inspection flagged two things to keep an eye on this winter: surface wear on the upper section and a culvert at the 0.4-mile mark. Neither is urgent — but if we get a particularly wet winter, the board may need to act sooner than spring. Marcus will check both after any significant storms.

What's Next

The next board meeting is March 15 at 10 am via video call. Members are welcome to attend. Contact the board if you'd like the call link.
