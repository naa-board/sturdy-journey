Meeting Summary March 20, 2025

March 2025 Board Meeting

This is an AI-generated summary of the board meeting. For the complete official record, see the full meeting minutes.

The Short Version

The board approved a spring pothole repair contract, reviewed the February storm cleanup costs, and confirmed that 18 of 24 parcels have paid their 2025 dues.

Road Work Coming in April

The board voted unanimously to hire Valley Road Services to patch the two potholes on the upper switchback and address cracking in two road sections. Work is scheduled for the week of April 21. Expect occasional single-lane traffic during morning hours — no full road closures are planned. Marcus will send a reminder email closer to the date.

Storm Cleanup Bill

The February slide cleanup cost $1,850. The board covered it from reserves but is watching full-year expenses before deciding whether a special assessment is needed. They'll revisit this at the fall meeting. Short version: no extra charges for now.

Dues Update

75% of parcels have paid 2025 dues. If you haven't yet, please do — the board is sending reminders to the remaining six parcels this month. Outstanding dues affect the association's ability to plan and pay for road work.

What's Next

The next board meeting will be in June 2025. The date will be announced by email. If you have something you'd like added to the agenda, email board@aldercroftheights.org at least two weeks in advance.
